Delete your account

Folio by Sohanuzzaman Soad

Deleting your Folio account removes, immediately and permanently:

  • your account (email, name and password);
  • your plan and its history, promo codes and requests;
  • your signed-in devices and monthly usage counts;
  • everything synced to Folio Cloud: books, covers, progress, highlights, notes and settings.

Books and notes stored on your device stay there; uninstall the app to remove them. Nothing is kept after deletion.

In the app

Open Settings → Server, scroll to Delete account, tap Delete and confirm with your password.

Here, on the web

Sign in to confirm it's your account.

Signed up with Google only, or can't sign in? Email mdsoad@gmail.com from your account's address and we'll delete it within 7 days.
