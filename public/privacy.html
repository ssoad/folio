Folio.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Folio is an e-book reader for Android, desktop and the web. This policy explains what information Folio handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: your books and notes stay on your device unless you choose to sync them, Folio has no ads, and it doesn't track you.

On your device

The books you add, their covers, your reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes and settings are stored on your device. Reading, organising and annotating books works without an account and without sending any of this anywhere.

Your Folio account (optional)

AI features, AI voices, text recognition (OCR), book information and Folio Cloud sync come from the Folio server at folio.armorclub.org and need a Folio account. When you create one, we keep:

  • your email address and the name you give;
  • your password, stored only as a salted hash (PBKDF2-SHA256), never in readable form;
  • the devices signed in to your account (a device name, such as "Folio Android", and when it signed in), so you can sign them out;
  • your plan, its history, promo codes you redeem and requests you send us;
  • monthly usage counts for limited features (number of AI requests, characters read aloud, pages recognised), which plans use for their limits.

Folio Cloud sync (optional)

If your plan includes Folio Cloud and you turn it on, Folio uploads your library to the Folio server: book files, covers, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes and settings. Each account's data is kept in its own file, encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key that is unique to the account, including the names of the files. The server holds the keys so that it can serve your data back to your devices.

If you sync to your own storage instead (WebDAV, S3 and others), your books go straight to that storage. Folio's server only encrypts the sign-in details for it, which then stay on your device.

AI, voices and text recognition

When you use an AI feature (asking about a passage, translating, dictionary help, recognising a book's title), Folio sends the text you chose (or a page image, for text recognition) to the Folio server. The server passes it to an AI model provider to produce the answer and returns the result. These providers currently include Mistral AI and NVIDIA, who handle it under their API terms. AI voices are generated on the Folio server itself. The Folio server doesn't store the text or images you send once the answer is returned, and we don't use them to train models.

Other services you choose to use

  • Book information: when you look up a book's details, its title and author are sent to Open Library.
  • Look-ups: a word or passage you look up in a dictionary, Wikipedia or a web search is sent to that service, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Catalogs (OPDS): catalogs you add are contacted directly from your device.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Security

Everything between the app and the Folio server travels over HTTPS. The server sits behind Cloudflare, which carries the traffic to it. Like any web service, it handles your IP address to deliver the traffic and to limit repeated sign-in attempts; we don't store it with your account.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time, in the app under Settings → Server → Delete account, or on the web at folio.armorclub.org/delete-account. Deleting it removes your account, your plan, your signed-in devices, your usage counts and everything synced to Folio Cloud, immediately and permanently. Books and notes on your device are not affected; you can remove them by uninstalling the app.

Children

Folio isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can see your account information in the app, change your password, sign out devices and delete your account yourself. For anything else (a copy of your data, a correction, or a question), email us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
