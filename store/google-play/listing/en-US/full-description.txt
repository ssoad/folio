Folio is a calm, modern reader for the books you already own. Add your EPUBs, PDFs and comics, and read them on clean pages that you can shape to your eyes, with your highlights and notes kept alongside.

YOUR LIBRARY, BEAUTIFULLY KEPT
• Covers, reading progress and "continue reading" at a glance
• Shelves and favourites to organise your books
• Filter by unread, reading and finished; search your whole library
• Grid, list or detailed views

READ THE WAY YOU LIKE
• Light, sepia, dark and black themes
• Adjustable text size, scrolling or page turns, and brightness
• A distraction-free page: tap to show or hide the controls
• Search inside a book, and jump through its contents

HIGHLIGHTS AND NOTES
• Highlight passages in colour with a tap
• Add notes, bookmarks, and copy or look up any word or passage
• See every highlight and note in one place
• Draw and annotate on PDF pages

LISTEN, ASK, TRANSLATE
• Read aloud with your phone's own voices, offline
• Natural AI voices with a Folio account
• An AI helper to explain, summarise or translate a passage
• Text recognition for scanned PDFs

EVERY FORMAT YOU READ
EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3, AZW, FB2, TXT, Markdown, DOCX, HTML, and comics in CBZ, CBR, CBT and CB7. Open books straight from your Files app, Downloads or email with "Open with Folio", or browse OPDS catalogs.

ON EVERY DEVICE, AND PRIVATE
• Folio Cloud keeps your books, notes, highlights and reading position in sync across devices, encrypted on our servers, with each account's data stored and encrypted separately
• Or sync to storage you run yourself, such as WebDAV or S3
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics

ACCOUNTS AND PLANS
Reading, organising and annotating your books needs no account. A free Folio account adds a monthly allowance of AI requests, AI voices and text recognition, plus book information look-ups. Folio Cloud sync is part of the Pro plan.

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time, in the app or at folio.armorclub.org/delete-account.
